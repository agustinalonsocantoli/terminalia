# Support

- **Questions and setup help:** see the [support FAQ](https://terminalia.dev/en/support).
- **Bug reports:** use the contact form on the [support page](https://terminalia.dev/en/support) and pick "I found a bug". Include your macOS version and the Terminalia version.
- **Feature requests:** use the form on the [changelog page](https://terminalia.dev/en/changelog).

Issues are disabled on this repository; every request goes through the website.
